Our 3D foot scanners

Our laser scanners capture the shape of the foot in 3D. The XPOD-HD350 scans the whole foot, and the XSOL-X2 captures the sole and arch. Both can scan with no, partial, or full weight on the foot, giving us digital models for foot assessment and custom orthotic design.
